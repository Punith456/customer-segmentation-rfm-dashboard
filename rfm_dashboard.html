Key insights

What the analysis surfaced
01

Revenue is concentrated in a small core

Champions and Loyal Customers are just 29% of the customer base but generate 53% of total revenue — the clearest signal to protect this group with a dedicated loyalty tier.

02

678 high-value customers were quietly slipping away

The At Risk segment showed high historical monetary value but sharply declining frequency — a pattern invisible in raw sales totals, only visible once R, F and M were scored separately.

03

New customers need a faster second purchase

456 New Customers had a single order and no repeat within 60 days — informing a targeted onboarding email flow to move them toward Potential Loyalist status.

04

Segmented outreach outperformed blanket campaigns

Replacing a single generic newsletter with segment-specific offers (win-back credit for At Risk, exclusive early access for Champions) drove the 15% retention improvement.
